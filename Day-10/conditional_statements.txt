1.simple if:it has one condition and one response
example:Google Pay, Instagram, Netflix, AI Chatbot
2.if-else: it has one condition and two response
example:PhonePe, Uber, WhatsApp, Amazon
3.elif ladder:it has more than 1 condition and more than 1 response
example:Netflix, Zomato, AI Resume Screening, Online Learning
4.nested if:it has one condition in another condition
example:Face ID, AI Job Portal, Online Gaming, Smart Home
